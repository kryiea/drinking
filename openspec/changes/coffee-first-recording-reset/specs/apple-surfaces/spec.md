# Apple Surfaces Spec

## Requirements
- The app must support iCloud-based sync for user settings and intake records within the Apple ecosystem.
- The iCloud sync path must be isolated behind a provider seam so the app can fall back to local-only mode when capability, account, or signing conditions are unavailable.
- The app must provide a simplified `我的` page focused on personal settings and app settings.
- The product plan must define at least one Widget surface for current caffeine state and one quick-log Widget surface before implementation starts.
- The product plan must define a watchOS companion app focused on quick logging and current caffeine status, not full catalog management.
